# Dictation benchmark — 2026-08-12

## Method

- MacBook Pro, macOS 26.5.2
- Built-in speakers to built-in microphone; identical Samantha voice corpus
- Five post-warm-up runs per clip
- Primary metric: hotkey release to visible text in a native `NSTextView`
- Accuracy: normalized word error rate (case and punctuation ignored)
- Listen 2.0.6 and Wispr Flow 1.5.433 used their configured cloud providers

## Results

| Product | Clip | Median | Range | WER |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Listen 2.0.6 | 2.24 s short | 1,329 ms | 1,142–1,493 | 14.3% |
| Listen 2.0.7 adaptive | 2.24 s short | 814 ms | 633–1,082 | 14.3% |
| Wispr Flow 1.5.433 | 2.24 s short | 729 ms | 602–798 | 14.3% |
| Listen 2.0.6 | 10.15 s medium | 1,317 ms | 1,220–2,311 | 0% |
| Listen 2.0.7 adaptive | 10.15 s medium | 898 ms | 778–1,124 | 0% |
| Wispr Flow 1.5.433 | 10.15 s medium | 1,210 ms | 1,089–1,468 | 0% |
| Listen 2.0.6 | 14.83 s technical | 1,473 ms | 1,454–1,853 | 0% |
| Listen 2.0.7 adaptive | 14.83 s technical | 1,000 ms | 891–1,448 | 0% |
| Wispr Flow 1.5.433 | 14.83 s technical | 1,208 ms | 1,156–1,320 | 0% |

Listen 2.0.7 is 39% faster than 2.0.6 on short speech, 32% faster on medium
speech, and 32% faster on technical speech. It is 312 ms faster than Wispr on
the medium clip and 208 ms faster on the technical clip. Wispr remains 85 ms
faster on the short clip. Both products made the same one-word acoustic error
on the short clip (`lunch` → `launch`).

## Optimization

Profiling separated the stages and found clean-up was a redundant serial cloud
request costing about 0.53 seconds after already-clean speech recognition.
Listen 2.0.7 locally detects clean prose and adds terminal punctuation without
that request. Filler, corrections, repeated words, lowercase fragments, and
long unstructured speech still take the full configured cleanup path.

## Superwhisper 2.11.0

Superwhisper was installed and tested, but no latency result is reported. Its
process repeatedly remained inside a synchronous scan of the local recordings
directory (1,025 entries) during startup for more than four minutes, never
becoming ready to accept either its documented toggle shortcut or documented
`superwhisper://record` deep link. Counting the resulting timeouts as dictation
latency would misrepresent the product; the cold-start failure is recorded
separately.
